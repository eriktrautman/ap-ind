Disclaimer
All numbers shown are placeholders (test data or September polling). Photos are placeholders. Gold marks the independent in every graphic; party colours are deliberately muted so the featured candidate is the first thing the eye lands on.
Graphics notes
This is television. Every graphic keeps its content inside the title-safe area (10% in from each side, 9% top and bottom; the "title-safe guide" toggle in the nav draws it on each sketch), uses one headline number per idea, and sets nothing smaller than 28px at 1080p. Where a sketch is dense, the full-size version shows fewer items at the same minimum size.
Data Audit
Every card below carries a data line. AP straight from the feed · AP-derived computed from AP fields only · mixed AP plus a dataset supplied by the project · outside data the chart cannot be made from the feed; a dataset that must be built and verified (card outlined orange).

What the AP feed provides (verified Oct 21): per-candidate votes at state and county level; precincts reporting and AP's expected-vote estimate (eevp) per unit; race calls, winner and call time; seat flips; incumbency and party; ranked-choice rounds; poll closing times; and past elections (2020, 2022, 2024) at the same detail, which covers every "vs last time" comparison. The reports feed (/v3/reports, section F) adds balance-of-power tables, poll hours by district, the filed-candidate roster, vote-type and advance-voting rules, 2024 count timing and party names.

Outside datasets to build and confirm:
  1. Pre-election polling for the seven featured races (B2, B3, C7)
  2. Candidate bios, photos and photo rights (A2, B1, B2, B2 ✓; candidate index in progress)
  3. Sourced quotes (B11)
  4. Current Senate composition of the 65 seats not up this cycle — now from the AP Trend report (F1)
  5. History of independents in Congress and best independent results before 2020 (C4, C5)
  6. Movement statistics: independent filings count (FEC), identification polling such as Gallup (C2, C3)
  7. AP VoteCast, a separate AP product not covered by the key (B12)
  8. Editorial context lines written by producers during the night (B4 targets, D6)
