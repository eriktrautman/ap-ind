How to Use This Site
    Chart Display Settings

    Live Ticker

    The Live Ticker is shared by everyone at a fixed URL so it can be kept up on a single laptop and hot-swapped for other crawls. The draft can be viewed at the URL generated by its selected options.

    Address for this ticker

    Use this ticker by its address, which carries every parameter and so never changes and needs no sign-in; or publish it to become the live ticker.

    Crawl order

    Races, text and chunks, in crawl order. ▲▼ reorder. A chunk shows its contents; hover a piece for the full text.

    Or add races from the Race Catalog below, and chunks from the Chunks area under this ticker. With nothing to crawl, the featured races crawl.

    Idle text

    Crawls on its own when the ticker has no races and no crawler text to show.

    Permanent Address:

    Picks up a publish within about 5 seconds at the next item boundary, without restarting the crawl; a note appears above while the live address has not picked one up yet. Only an authorized publish changes it.

    Chunks

    A chunk is a package of races and text, built here once and placed in the crawl order above as one element, as many times as you like. Chunks travel with the ticker: Publish sends them with the crawl, Pull brings the live ones back and keeps any you have only here.

    Live Screens

    Live Screens are shared by everyone at a fixed URL so they can be kept up on a single laptop and hot-swapped for other graphics. The drafts can be viewed at the URLs generated by their selected options.

    Slate

    Featuring a race only makes its charts easy to grab here. It changes nothing in the ticker or graphics.

    Races on the slate

    Or feature any race from the Race Catalog below; pick the charts from the Chart Catalog.

    Featured charts · the same set for every featured race

    Or add from the Chart Catalog below.

    Races

    Where the charts for election-night coverage of many races at once live: Senate and House maps, balance of power, key races, zoom-ins, the popular vote, too close to call (the D series). Add from the Chart Catalog; remove from a card's ⋯ menu.

    Movement

    The independent movement as a whole rather than any one race: the featured-races board, the national independent vote, polls against results (the C series). Add from the Chart Catalog; remove from a card's ⋯ menu.

    Persistent Elements

    Lower thirds and strips (the A series). Add from the Chart Catalog; each one takes its inputs here; remove from a card's ⋯ menu. The live ticker has its own Live Ticker section.

    Race Catalog

    Every race in the AP feed. Add any to the ticker or feature it; expand a race for all of its graphics.
